## Verdict

**CHANGES REQUIRED.** The visual direction is now right and I approve it. Ten blocking findings remain, mostly MVP flows from the PRD that have no screen yet, plus one visual that breaks the founder's no-dash rule. Fix these and send it back for review 02.

## What is approved (keep it)

- Type system: Bricolage Grotesque for the persona's voice and headings, Geist for the app, mono only for numbers. It reads as a 2026 premium consumer app; nothing looks like a serif or anything from before the 2020s.
- Geometric persona marks (chevron, grid, waves). Telling the personas apart never depends on colour alone, there is no likeness risk, and they swap cleanly with config.
- The naming rule (design-system section 10) fixes the "Harvey SHARP" bug. Every frame uses the full name only on hero surfaces and the first name everywhere else, and intensity always sits in its own pill reading "Intensity: Sharp".
- The voice mode choreography: listening, thinking, then speaking with captions that are always on. "Tap anywhere to interrupt" is the right pattern.
- Error, offline and loading states are inline, never modal, and keep the user's words.
- The wordmark and icon work at 18px and in the PWA mask.

## Journey walkthrough (after fixes, this is the expected path)

1. **First run:** splash, What it is, Before we start (18+ and disclosure), Meet the three, Home as guest with example card and quick situations.
2. **First consult by voice:** tap talk, mic priming sheet, OS prompt, listening (tap again or pause to finish), thinking, speaking with captions, result. Then the "Keep this case?" sheet, then sign in or Not now.
3. **Follow-up:** push back by dock or by interrupting in voice mode, then the thread with the new reply performing; the When chip and feedback row are available.
4. **Persona switch:** "Ask Donna" on the result or the composer chip in the thread, then a divider and Donna's reply in her tint; "See Donna's take" afterwards.
5. **Return visit:** splash, Home with recent cases and a debrief card if a moment has passed, debrief chips, and the case continues.
6. **Limit hit:** the next consult shows the weekly limit screen, then the paywall stub sheet. Saved lines and past cases still open. Care mode is never gated.
7. **Delete account:** Settings, Privacy, optional Download my data, Delete account sheet, typed confirmation, then the landing with the "data deleted" toast.
